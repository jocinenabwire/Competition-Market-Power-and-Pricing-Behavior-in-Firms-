Competition and Firm Performance

Main outcome:
The main dependent variable is log sales per worker, used as a proxy for firm productivity.

Competition measure:
The main explanatory variable is competition_intensity, coded from 0 to 3 according to the number of competitors.

Sample sizes:
Productivity sample: 24103
Full productivity sample: 10672
Sales robustness sample: 10672
Export sample: 26254
Innovation sample: 11588
Capacity utilization sample: 25580
Price increase sample: 14091

Empirical strategy:
The main specification uses country fixed effects and sector controls. Firm size categories are excluded from regressions because log employment controls for firm scale. More demanding fixed effects are reported as robustness checks.

Identification warning:
Competition is not randomly assigned. More productive firms may select into more competitive markets, and unobserved product quality may affect both competition exposure and productivity. Therefore, results should be interpreted as conditional associations rather than strict causal effects.
