High Competition and Firm Performance

Main idea:
The main competition variable is a binary indicator equal to one if the firm reports more than five competitors, and zero if the firm reports zero to five competitors.

Sample sizes:
Sales sample: 24103
Full-control sales sample: 10672
Productivity sample: 24103
Export participation sample: 26378
Innovation sample: 11588
Capacity utilization sample: 25693
Price increase sample: 14091

Empirical strategy:
The main outcome is log sales. The main specification uses country fixed effects and sector controls, because competition is partly sectoral. More demanding fixed effects are treated as robustness checks. Firm size categories are excluded from regressions because log employment controls for firm scale.

Identification warning:
Competition is not randomly assigned. More productive firms may select into more competitive markets, and unobserved product quality may affect both competition exposure and firm performance. Therefore, results should be interpreted as conditional associations rather than strict causal effects.
