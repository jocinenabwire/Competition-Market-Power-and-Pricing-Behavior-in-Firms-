High Competition and Firm Performance

Main idea:
The main competition variable is a binary indicator equal to one if the firm reports more than five competitors, and zero if the firm reports zero to five competitors.

Sample sizes:
Sales sample: 24103
Full-control sales sample: 10672
Productivity sample: 24103
Full-control productivity sample: 10672
Export participation sample: 26378
Price increase sample: 14091

Empirical strategy:
The main outcome is log sales. The main explanatory variable is high competition. The baseline model controls for sector and country fixed effects. Additional specifications include log employment, multi-establishment status, firm age and manager experience. Informal competition is excluded from the main regression table but retained in the robustness component model.

Winsorisation note:
Sales are winsorised using fixed bounds: values below 1,000 are set to 1,000, and values above 1,000,000 are set to 1,000,000. Sales per worker is computed from the winsorised sales variable and is not winsorised again.

Alternative outcomes note:
The alternative outcome table reports productivity, export participation and price increase. Capacity utilization and innovation are excluded from the main alternative outcome table.

Identification warning:
Competition is not randomly assigned. More productive firms may select into more competitive markets, and unobserved product quality may affect both competition exposure and firm performance. Therefore, results should be interpreted as conditional associations rather than strict causal effects.
