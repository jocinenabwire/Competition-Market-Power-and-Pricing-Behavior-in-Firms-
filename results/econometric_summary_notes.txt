Competitive Pressure and Firm Performance

Main idea:
The main competition variable is a PCA-based index using number of competitors, whether the firm competes with informal firms, and perceived obstacle from informal competitors.

Sample sizes:
Sales sample: 15235
Full-control sales sample: 9968
Productivity sample: 15235
Export participation sample: 16829
Innovation sample: 5672
Capacity utilization sample: 16248

Empirical strategy:
The main outcome is log sales. Additional outcomes are log sales per worker, capacity utilization, export participation, and innovation. The main specification uses country fixed effects and sector controls, because competition is partly sectoral. More demanding fixed effects are treated as robustness checks.

Identification warning:
Competition is not randomly assigned. More productive firms may select into more competitive markets, and unobserved product quality may affect both competition exposure and firm performance. Therefore, results should be interpreted as conditional associations rather than strict causal effects.
